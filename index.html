Dr. Wei Chen is a tenured Associate Professor at Shenzhen Technology University (SZTU). He leads the Energy & Photonics Lab (EPL) and Lab for Smart Optoelectronic Integration Systems (SOIS). He holds a B.Sc. in Physics from Hubei Engineering University, an M.Sc. in Materials Science from Hubei University, and a Ph.D. (Dr.rer.nat.) from the Technical University of Munich (TUM), Germany.

Dr. Chen's research focuses on solution-processed semiconductor thin films, including colloidal quantum dots (CQDs), perovskites, and organic semiconductors, as well as smart optoelectronic integration systems. His work connects materials science, device physics, and system integration. He collaborates with academic and industry partners to develop next-generation optoelectronic devices and systems.

Before joining SZTU in 2022, Dr. Chen conducted research on CQD-based optoelectronic applications at the Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech). He was promoted to Associate Professor in 2024.

At SZTU, Dr. Chen teaches undergraduate courses in solid-state physics and semiconductor physics. He mentors students and welcomes those interested in materials science and photonic technologies to engage with the EPL and SOIS labs.
